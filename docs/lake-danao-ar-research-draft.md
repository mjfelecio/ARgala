# Lake Danao AR story — research and editorial draft

Research date: 2026-11-09. **Draft for review; not approved destination content.**
This record is not a catalog, entrance, translation, media or AR admission approval.
All statements below refer to Lake Danao in Ormoc, Leyte, not the namesake lakes.

## Editorial direction

**A living lake:** let the visitor encounter the highland landscape, then discover
its aquatic life, the people connected to it and its protection. Short visual
sequences should invite attention to the real place, not imply that photographs
are current views or that the device recognizes the surroundings.

### Suggested chapters and draft English copy

**The lake and its landscape**

> Lake Danao sits among the uplands of Ormoc, surrounded by forested terrain.
> Seen from above, its outline has been described as guitar-shaped. The lake and
> the land around it form a protected natural park.

Sources: [Ormoc City project description](https://r8.emb.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/NOTICE_PDS-Lake-Danao-Natural-Park.pdf),
[1998 protected-area proclamation](https://ldr.senate.gov.ph/subject/lake-danao).
Proposed imagery: an identifiable, properly credited lake view and a distinct
view of its surrounding landscape. Do not present a ground photograph as an aerial.

**Life beneath the surface**

> Researchers working with local fishers studied the fish of Lake Danao from
> 2021 to 2022. Their study recorded eight species and examined how local people
> use fish as food. It also called for continued monitoring of the lake's
> aquatic life.

Source: [Romero et al., 2023, National Fisheries Research and Development Institute](https://repository.nfrdi.da.gov.ph/items/831d2e6a-1789-44d7-9d88-6a9698ac179e).
These are findings from a defined study period, not a current species inventory.
Proposed imagery: locally verified photographs of fish or research work; none
identified with sufficient provenance yet. Do not substitute a generic species image.

**People and water**

> For some households around Lake Danao, fishing is connected to food and
> livelihood. Researchers have studied fish catches and water quality to support
> decisions about both communities and the lake's biodiversity.

Source: [National Research Council of the Philippines, FiBRe project](https://basicresearch.nrcp.dost.gov.ph/supported_programs_and_projects/details/308),
which describes its 2020–2022 project period; do not imply the project is currently
running. Proposed imagery: a locally supplied photograph with subject consent and
context. A boat photo alone does not establish who uses it or their livelihood.

**A protected place**

> Lake Danao and its surroundings were declared a protected natural park in 1998.
> Caring for the lake involves its water, wildlife and the communities connected
> to it.

Sources: [Senate proclamation record](https://ldr.senate.gov.ph/subject/lake-danao),
[FiBRe project](https://basicresearch.nrcp.dost.gov.ph/supported_programs_and_projects/details/308).
Proposed imagery: verified images of the landscape and conservation work; avoid
inventing historical photographs, rules or present-day conservation outcomes.

## Decisions and evidence needed before release

- Client/editor review of every story and image; sufficient section-relevant
  imagery for a visual sequence, with exact content version and approval evidence.
- Confirm destination center and accessible entrance separately, plus current
  admission policy with the relevant local authority; research photo coordinates
  must not become entry or navigation authority.
- Review English and provide/review Japanese and Korean content, captions and alt
  text. The existing approval contract cannot be satisfied by invented translations.
- Reconcile measurements before displaying them: the city document reports about
  148 hectares while the fisheries paper cites 139.8 hectares. Visitor rules,
  hours and access conditions must be freshly confirmed rather than inferred.
- Verify native rendering, actual glyphs, accessibility, tracking and touch on the
  physical device and geographic admission on site. A specific APK build requires
  separate owner approval.
