# ARgala location field guide

**For the field pass, test the Directions entrance and the on-site AR zone as two stops.** At the entrance, look for entrance guidance and the optional arrival notice. At a permitted point well inside the destination polygon, look for the verified unlock and use **Start AR**. The display pin, entrance point, Android reminder and AR polygon can be in different places. D10 (ACLC College of Ormoc) has an approximate Directions entrance and a separately approved polygon. Check school permission before entering.

## Run sheet

Use the same Android build for the series. Note the device, OS, build, approximate location grant versus precise grant, and whether location services were enabled. Start the device pass outdoors with an unobstructed sky and point the camera at a well-lit, textured scene.

| Destination | Permission / entrance landmark | Seconds to usable watcher fix | Entrance notice and distance | Seconds to AR unlock from first inside fix | Boundary / fix quality / decision if delayed | Tracking and tap result |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| D__ | | | | | | |

1. From outside, open the destination in Explore or Directions. Check that the public guidance changes as you approach and Start AR stays unavailable outside. In a development build, set `EXPO_PUBLIC_VIRO_SPIKE=1` in the Metro environment before starting it. Open **Location diagnostics** next to the featured arrival controls. Use the normal destination entry, without the simulation action, for this pass.
2. Opt in to the foreground entrance notice. Stop at the intended entrance and record displayed distance, fix quality and whether the notice appeared. Test the optional background Android reminder in a separate pass with notification and background-location permission. Start outside its region and allow for Android delivery delay.
3. Walk to the permitted interior stop. Hold still long enough for multiple watcher callbacks and record the first usable inside fix and the unlock. A GPS dot can appear before the continuous watcher has usable evidence. The fastest qualifying pair spans at least three seconds. Measure actual delay rather than treating the requested watcher interval as a delivery guarantee.
4. Try the boundary from both sides, then go sufficiently far outside and return. Check that minor jitter does not replay the celebration. Compare a cold start to a return from background. Try camera permission on a fresh test session and record the difference between waiting for geographic eligibility, waiting for camera permission, and waiting for native tracking.
5. Once live, walk a short safe distance off the test point, then suspend and recover the app. Check recovery within the grace period and a fresh launch after expiry. On a registered account test an earned Passport stamp through the normal flow. Keep demo and verified evidence separate.
6. In Nearby Places, use a test account and explicitly scan outdoors. Turn slowly with the phone upright, tap a floating card, open results, and return to AR. Repeat with weak heading or tracking loss, then recover. Check long names in EN/JA/KO, bright and low-light readability, and real finger taps. One known device observation is tracking loss facing a featureless wall. [Prior device evidence](validation.md#d10-device-verification-over-adb--2026-11-27)
7. For the presentation indoors, try **Profile → AR Showcase → destination → Start AR** against a textured, well-lit scene. Describe the Demo Passport as the showcase record. Have the browser preview ready to explain Nearby layout independently of GPS, account access and live camera tracking.

## Systems and knobs

### Watcher and verified arrival

- **Foreground GPS** requests high accuracy, a 3 s time interval and normally 5 m distance interval. Explore, featured Directions, general AR and featured Nearby request 0 m so they can receive updates while stationary. Startup, including permission and service checks, is bounded by 15 s. Native delivery can vary. Foreground ownership, watcher health and sample timestamps are separate from the latest displayed position. A one-shot fix cannot restore watcher health or corroborate arrival. [Limits](../src/config/limits.ts) · [Location service](../src/features/location/service.ts) · [Foreground owner](../src/features/location/use-device-location.ts)
- **Verified admission** evaluates the raw reported fix against the selected destination’s approved polygon or multipolygon. The outer boundary counts as contained and hole interiors and edges are excluded. The accuracy ceiling filters fix quality. Containment still uses the reported point. The watcher must be healthy. Samples must be at most 10 s old, no more than 1 s in the future, with a finite accuracy from 0 to 50 m. The engine requires at least two different timestamps spanning at least 3 s. A changed fix at the same timestamp, out-of-order timestamp, missing evidence or jump exceeding `30 m + 12 m/s × elapsed seconds` clears the corroborating run. A gap over 10 s restarts it. The guarded legacy `pilot-aclc` diagnostic alone uses a 50 m circle. [Proximity](../src/features/destinations/proximity.ts) · [Geometry](../src/features/destinations/ar-geofences.ts) · [Approved geometry and mapping](../src/features/destinations/data/)
- **Approach and repeat visits** use distance plus reported accuracy for the 500 m approaching and 150 m nearby signals. An outside fix withdraws current entry eligibility. A witnessed exit clears celebration deduplication only after two corroborating readings over 3 s whose distance outside exceeds reported accuracy plus 10 m. Launch-ready content is an additional requirement. Diagnostic rows show the most recent model decision, current fix age, the accepted fix’s boundary relationship when evaluated, and the inside run. Repeated means the existing timestamp contributed no new evidence. The panel uses the active screen’s source and does not save or send readings. [Admission store](../src/features/destination-ar/admission-store.ts)
- **Native entry** starts with an explicit Start AR. The single-use entry is valid for 30 s, with geographic revalidation before first mounting and through initial tracking-ready. Native support, camera permission, scene geometry and tracking readiness are separate. The startup owner bounds an attempt with a 20 s deadline. A failed or interrupted initial start needs fresh eligibility and an explicit retry. Once native tracking-ready establishes admission, moving out of the polygon does not end it. Temporary tracking loss in a surviving frame retains the exhibit orientation. Suspension gives up to 5 min to recover the live session. [Entry intent](../src/features/destination-ar/entry-intent.ts) · [Native startup](../src/features/destination-ar/use-viro-startup.ts) · [Session](../src/features/destination-ar/session.ts)

### Entrance, reminder and walking

- **Foreground entrance notice** uses the approved Directions point and an active foreground watcher. Opt in for the destination. A raw fix within 20 m with reported accuracy ≤50 m begins confirmation. It uses the shared 10 s age, 1 s future tolerance and movement plausibility checks, plus 3 s of time-separated evidence. To rearm, distance from the entrance minus accuracy must reach at least 65 m and be corroborated. [Entrance logic](../src/features/destinations/entrance-arrival.ts)
- **Android nearby reminder** uses a separate 150 m OS region centered on its configured reminder point (activation point when present, otherwise the display center). It requires per-destination consent and Android permissions. It is one-time per selection, up to 10 destinations, and best effort. Android controls delivery and latency. [Reminder model](../src/features/arrival-reminders/model.ts)
- **Directions preview** uses the explicit route intent and a current foreground origin. Both latest position and continuous watcher timestamps must be current for the owner, ≤30 s old and ≤1 s in the future, with accuracy ≤100 m. The preview intent lifetime is 2 min. The submitted origin and destination are fixed for that route preview. [Preview](../src/features/directions/use-route-preview.ts) · [Intent](../src/features/directions/route-preview-intent.ts)
- **In-app walking progress** needs an explicitly started walk and matching continuous fix and watcher timestamps, age ≤10 s, and accuracy ≤25 m. Samples must be at least 1 s apart. Matching uses a corridor of `max(20 m, 1.5 × accuracy)` and a 3 m/s motion budget with accuracy allowance. Two fixes acquire progress and three outside the matching corridor establish off-route. Parallel/overlapping segments and large jumps withhold progress. Along-route steps cap at 75 m and large forward moves need corroboration. Arrival takes two fixes with ≤10 m accuracy and both straight-line and remaining route distance ≤15 m. Stop restores the full route. [Walking logic](../src/features/directions/walking-progress.ts)

### Browse and the two Nearby camera experiences

- **Explore search** can use a live center with the default 1 km radius or an explicit viewport search, capped at 5 km. A retained or browse center can show the map without qualifying as live GPS. **Featured destination establishments** search around the destination center within 500 m. These are discovery radii. [Explore](../src/features/map/map-screen.tsx) · [Viewport sizing](../src/features/map/search-state.ts) · [Featured establishments](../src/features/destinations/nearby.ts)
- **General AR** projects ordinary POI labels into an approximate 60° horizontal field, up to three labels. Its settings radius is 100 m by default and adjustable from 10 to 1,000 m. Fresh GPS (≤30 s, ≤100 m accuracy and healthy watcher) determines distance and activation. Projection also needs a true heading with quality ≥2 and age ≤10 s. The 10° degree readout and 8° hold threshold affect the readout only. Cached search reuse is bounded by 2 min and 100 m from its source center. [General AR](../src/features/ar/ar-discovery.tsx) · [Projection](../src/features/ar/projection.ts) · [Reuse](../src/features/ar/use-ar-places.ts)
- **Nearby inside featured AR and Showcase** needs sign-in and an explicit Scan nearby using the current foreground watcher sample (≤30 s, ≤100 m accuracy, and ≤1 s in the future). Search radius is 1 km with up to 20 deduplicated results. Results expire after 2 min. Camera geometry is recalculated from the latest usable fix. The results view holds the origin captured when it was opened for its displayed distances. It can remain usable during heading loss, and hides distances if opened without a usable origin. Opening it temporarily removes the native camera scene. [Scan and placement inputs](../src/features/destination-ar/nearby-mode.ts) · [Results and scan lifecycle](../src/features/destination-ar/use-nearby-mode.ts) · [Session flow](../src/features/destination-ar/session-experience.tsx)
- **Floating cards** use straight-line distance and compass bearing. Preferred place goes first, then closest, then provider ID for ties. A place under 1 m is excluded from spatial rendering. The allocator tries lanes at `0, +0.48, −0.48, +0.96, −0.96` world units, accepting the first lane with at least 15° clearance from every card in that lane. Every card is placed at 3.2 world units with width 0.78 and height 0.38. Distance changes the displayed label, not the ring depth. When all five lanes are busy around a bearing, that place remains accessible in the list. **Show card in AR** gives the place priority in allocation. Turn manually toward its bearing. [Shared allocator](../src/features/destination-ar/nearby-card-layout.ts)
- **Featured AR heading** uses Android rotation-vector heading with geomagnetic declination from the current GPS fix, medium-or-better sensor quality, and a reasonably level rear-camera vector. The native stream emits at most about every 100 ms. The JS pairing accepts heading aged −50 to 180 ms and a camera pose received within 250 ms. Acquisition needs at least three compatible pairs over at least 150 ms with gaps ≤400 ms and heading/offset variation ≤8°. Small corrections within 15° blend at 18%. Alignment expires after 1.2 s without a compatible refresh, and the no-reading state starts after 2.5 s. The ring translates with the current valid pose, throttled to 90 ms; look direction changes which cards the camera sees. [Heading alignment](../src/features/destination-ar/nearby-mode.ts) · [Native layer](../src/features/destination-ar/nearby-viro-layer.tsx) · [Android module](../modules/ar-camera-heading/android/src/main/java/com/argala/arheading/ArCameraHeadingModule.kt)

## Diagnose a field stall

| What you observe | What to investigate before changing a value |
| --- | --- |
| Accuracy exceeds 50 m or age exceeds 10 s at a known interior stop | Confirm Android precise location, services, continuous watcher health and sample cadence. Compare an open-sky point with the intended approach. Consider the accuracy ceiling or age only after observing stable, usable inside fixes and checking false unlocks outside. |
| Accuracy is good, but the intended entrance is consistently outside | Verify the entrance landmark, approved GeoJSON and public approach guidance. Record the mismatch for owner review. Preserve the polygon as admission authority. |
| Inside count repeatedly returns to zero | Compare boundary distance, actual sample timestamps, jumps and stale periods. Move farther inside to isolate boundary jitter. Revisit the 3 s run and cadence only after distinguishing those causes. |
| Eligible but Start AR fails, or the Passport does not advance | Check content readiness, the 30 s handoff, camera permission, native startup and tracking-ready. A GPS threshold change cannot fix a native tracking failure. |
| Entrance notice misses while verified AR works | Check opt-in, the entrance point and the 20 m approach rule. Tune the entrance threshold independently from the polygon after confirming the actual arrival path. |
| Scan works but cards are missing | Check list count, the camera-heading message, tracking state, and whether the target is near another place’s bearing. Hold the phone upright by a textured scene and turn slowly. Use results when heading is unavailable. |
| Walking route preview works but progress stalls | Check continuous sample accuracy, narrow or parallel routes, and the match versus arrival rules. Test a simple route before adjusting the walking corridor. |

## Open the browser preview

From the checkout, run `npm run preview:ar-nearby` and open the localhost URL it prints. Use **Current** to learn the existing placement and **Proposed** to test the new list and detail layouts or adjust the cards. In **Clustered bearings**, use **View results**, pick the sixth place, and select **Show card in AR** to see it gain a lane. Change the scenario to **Expired results** while a detail is open to test recovery.

Edit bearings and distances directly in **Place placement**. **Download settings** saves the synthetic fixture geometry, lighting, and layout controls locally. Import that file to reproduce changes in another browser on the same checkout. **Reset to app defaults** restores the geometry controls and current fixture’s original positions. Browser perspective, text clipping and sensor states are approximations for reviewing layout. Device checks establish actual Viro rendering, tracking, compass alignment and touch.
