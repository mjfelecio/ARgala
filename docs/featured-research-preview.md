# Featured destination research previews

Research snapshot: 2026-11-09. Draft material for the guarded development AR
preview, not verification of a destination, a route, entry eligibility or present
visitor arrangements. Preview entry is simulated, has development provenance and
never grants a Passport stamp. It is accessed from the existing development AR
entry in Profile when both development guards are enabled. English, Japanese and Korean story text is present as unreviewed draft material. Native device and on-site verification remain outstanding.

The source links support research leads, not blanket approval of current claims.
There is no reliable evidence here for prices, operating hours, accessibility,
permission to enter, current trail safety or independently verified entrances.
The preview does not import these coordinates into the live catalog. Video playback
remains blocked by the documented native seeking failure; all nine development overviews include research photos. The owner approved the existing resources for this personal preview; independently verifying creators, reuse rights and destination facts is separate from that approval. Do not represent this draft as a complete
or verified nine-destination experience.
