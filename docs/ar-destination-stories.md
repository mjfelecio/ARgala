# Destination Stories

Selected-destination AR uses the existing local Viro ring and captured entry
content. Before arrival, Explore and Directions retain minimal featured details.
Verified arrival offers an explicit launch; it never opens the camera automatically.

## Presentation

Chapter covers show their sequence, title and an Opened indicator. Overview is
ahead, History is to the right, and optional chapters occupy the remaining ring.
All 1–12 chapters accepted by the catalog can render. The square panels remain at
a 2.25-unit focused distance (owner-requested comfort adjustment on 2026-11-11). Native held interaction, tracking guards,
collapse-on-other-tile and explicit Recenter remain unchanged.

The reader leads with the first approved bound image, then complete text, then
additional images. Captions, alt text, credits and English fallback notices remain
paginated and reachable. Text-only stories have no empty image placeholder.
Long cover titles are abbreviated by grapheme; their full title is included in
the reading text. Images use more vertical space than the previous strip.
Ordinary paragraphs flow across the native reading box; page estimates do not
force narrow display rows. Explicit paragraphs and protective long-token/CJK
breaks remain intact. The expanded reader uses a separate clean matte texture, preserving the original
chapter-cover artwork. Text uses a 0.82-unit region and contained photographs a
0.76-unit region, with separate caption/credit space. Previous, page count, Next
and Close sit together in a floating native row beneath the content canvas; the
row shares its transition/frame owner and retires while opening or closing.

Exit, destination and Help occupy the top utility surface. Recenter and Capture
occupy a separate bounded bottom surface, alongside contextual A−/A+ while
a chapter is focused. The parent owns the single reading preference and its
localized error/Retry feedback; commands are fenced to that chapter and frame. Help scrolls within its visible card
and pauses native interaction without remounting the camera or pausing expiry.
Detailed stories and page/Close controls remain in Viro; only definite unsupported
hardware can enter the existing continuously eligible on-site reader.

## Progress and restoration

Opened means a chapter's accepted focus transition completed. Clicking another
tile to collapse a panel, stale native callbacks and restoring focus do not add
openings. Chapter progress is transient logical session state, not reading
completion or Passport eligibility. Exit, expiry and a new principal/admission
clear it. Eligible restoration retains it.

Text reflow uses source offsets within the same locale, independently of cover
page count. A locale change starts at the first text page because translated
offsets are not equivalent. Image reflow retains logical media ID and caption
page, clamped to available metadata. Session snapshots retain these image fields.
Existing text-only snapshots remain usable.

## Editorial delivery

The interface does not supply missing tourism facts. D01–D09 remain provisional
name-only entries, and the ACLC pilot has no bundled approved stories. Development
sample chapters demonstrate the structure and remain labeled. The ACLC research
draft and simulated admission retain their existing development gates.

Prepare one reviewed record per destination using the owner's research form:

- Confirm official name, destination point and accessible entrance separately.
- Supply Overview; add History, Highlights, Visitor Information and Interesting
  Facts only when supported. Keep paragraphs short and supply source/review evidence.
- Review EN/JA/KO translations independently. Unreviewed translations remain
  pending; bundled approved English may use the existing disclosed fallback.
- Date-check changing visitor information. Supply original photos, binding to
  chapter IDs, captions, alt text, visible credit and permission for bundled use.
- Keep draft content out of production. A complete remotely published text
  overlay requires all three reviewed locales under the existing protocol.

Ship one complete approved destination story first, verify it on site, then
expand to the remaining approved destinations. Text updates retain the explicit
Check for updates workflow and cannot install new media, locations or authority.
No new schema, endpoint, permissions or native dependencies are introduced.

## Acceptance

The November 12 refinement and current device limits are recorded in
[the implementation report](ar-reader-refinement-implementation.md).

Automated checks cover chapter capacity, focus/opening semantics, mixed-page
reflow, logical restoration, actual locales, Help fencing, failure recovery and
existing admission/Passport boundaries. Physical acceptance must separately cover
the rebuilt/current-compatible APK, native font/image fit, bright/dark camera
contrast, held hits, chrome touch routing, tracking recovery, GPS and unsupported
hardware. A browser sketch or mocked scene is not native acceptance evidence.

No APK build is included in this implementation. Obtain approval for a specific
build if the installed native capabilities cannot run the changes.
