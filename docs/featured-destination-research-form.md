# ARgala Featured Destination Research Form

Please prepare one copy of this form for each destination. The information will
be used for the destination listing, directions and the information panels shown
in AR.

Include a source for factual information. If something cannot be confirmed,
write **Pending verification** and note what still needs checking. Please do not
fill gaps with estimates or assumptions.

pedestrian and vehicle access, or any access restrictions.

need to choose a distance in this form.
