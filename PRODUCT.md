## Platform

android

## Users

Visitors discovering places and visiting selected featured destinations, including
English-, Japanese- and Korean-language users. Outdoor readability, limited GPS,
offline curated content and moderate Android hardware are recorded use contexts.
No age, demographic or accessibility research beyond the documented requirements
is asserted. [Source: blueprint §§1, 8–10](ARgala_MVP_Blueprint.md).

## Product Purpose

Unrestricted map discovery and explicit route previews coexist with a curated,
on-site destination AR experience. Preserve marker → bottom sheet → Directions
and selected-destination entry. Passport lives in Featured Destinations; Saved
Places is reached from Profile. Guests retain local progress; online paid
discovery/routes require authentication. [Blueprint D01–D30](ARgala_MVP_Blueprint.md),
[journal](docs/local-travel-journal.md), [navigation](docs/app-shell-navigation.md).

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the polished, Google Maps-inspired Clean Explorer identity. AR is a
restrained, coherent extension, not a new sci-fi shell. Use existing approved
assets and content only. [Blueprint D35, D47 and §8](ARgala_MVP_Blueprint.md).
